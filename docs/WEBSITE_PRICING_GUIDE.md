## 1. Executive Summary & Pricing Philosophy

Our website development services operate on a **transparent, fixed-scope tier model**. Rather than billing unpredictable hourly rates that leave clients uncertain about final costs, every package has a clearly defined scope based on four core pillars:

1. **Page Depth & Information Architecture:** The total number of bespoke, responsive pages designed and coded.
2. **Backend & Form Integrations:** From simple static contact links to validated custom forms with transactional email routing, anti-spam mechanisms, and Google Maps API embedding.
3. **Layout & Content Systems:** Proof and authority modules such as work showcases, filterable galleries, testimonial sliders, FAQ accordions, and scalable blog template architectures.
4. **Technical & SEO Baseline:** Mobile-first responsive QA across viewports, on-page SEO metadata structuring, image compression, and Core Web Vitals performance optimization.

### Canonical Currency & Global Pricing
- **Primary Source of Truth:** All contract quotes, invoices, and milestones are billed in **Philippine Peso (PHP)**.
- **International Invoicing:** USD, EUR, AUD, GBP, and SGD estimates are calculated using live institutional exchange rates (e.g., via the Frankfurter API on the website) and can be settled via Wise, direct bank wire, or credit card invoicing.

## 4. Tier Jump Rationale (Why Prices Increase Between Tiers)

When explaining quotes to prospective clients, use this simple sequence logic:

### 1. From ₱10,000 to ₱30,000 (The "Commercial Foundation Leap"):
- **Why the jump?** ₱10k is a *single-page showcase* without dynamic backend forms. The ₱30k Starter tier introduces commercial software architecture: server-side email routing, input validation, security sanitization, bespoke brand design, 3 standalone pages, and multi-device responsiveness.
- **Client Takeaway:** "₱10k gets you a live personal landing page; ₱30k gets your business a true lead-generating asset."

### 2. From ₱30,000 to ₱45,000 (+₱15,000):
- **Why the jump?** Adds 2 full standalone pages (Dedicated Services & Portfolio Gallery) plus two major technical integrations: Google Maps location embedding and advanced qualifying forms (budget/service selectors).
- **Client Takeaway:** "For an extra ₱15k, clients see your exact menu of services, visual proof of past results, and your physical presence on Google Maps."

### 3. From ₱45,000 to ₱60,000 (+₱15,000):
- **Why the jump?** Adds 3 full strategic pages (Blog Hub, FAQ accordion, Testimonials showcase) and a scalable article engine for compounding organic SEO.
- **Client Takeaway:** "For ₱15,000 more, you get an entire content engine, answer customer objections before they call, and showcase client reviews."

### 4. From ₱60,000 to ₱100,000–₱120,000 (The "Creative Tech & App Leap"):
- **Why the leap?** This moves from content publishing websites into full custom web applications: window management, real-time audio synthesis, dynamic AI persona integration, and headless cloud CMS engineering.
- **Client Takeaway:** "This is not just a website; it is an unforgettable flagship digital product that sets a new industry standard."
